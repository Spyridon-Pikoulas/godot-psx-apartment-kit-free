# PSX Apartment Kit Free

**15 free modular PS1-style pieces to build a condemned flat** for your survival horror game: carpet, a plaster ceiling, damask walls whole and torn, the flat's front door in its doorway, a sash window that slides up, a radiator, a kitchen sink, a bare bulb that swings, peeling paper, mould and a blood drag. They snap to a 2 m grid. Low-poly and point-filtered, a scene per piece with its colliders, ready to drop in. Free for commercial use.

### Want more?

**[PSX Apartment Kit](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-apartment-kit)** has 105 pieces and 21
animations for a whole apartment block: corridors, a stairwell up to the roof, a cage lift, a
laundry, bathrooms and kitchens, doors on their chains and kicked in, collapsed ceilings and holes
through the walls, 31 seamless textures, a Unity package and a demo level you can walk through in
your browser. Same addon, same look: it installs over this one. It shares
[PSX Hospital Kit](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-hospital-kit)'s grid, and
[PSX Hospital Kit Free](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-hospital-kit-free) is free too.

### What's inside

- **Structure:** a carpet floor, a plaster ceiling, a papered wall, a torn one, a window wall, a
  flat's entrance and a corner post, on a 2 m grid, 3 m high.
- **A flat's door** that swings open, its colliders moving with it, and **a sash window** whose
  lower sash slides up.
- **The flat:** a cast-iron radiator, a kitchen sink unit whose door opens, a bare bulb swinging
  on its flex.
- **Mess:** peeling wallpaper, black mould, a blood drag.
- **4 animations** on the moving parts, as clips in GLB and takes in FBX.
- **15 icons**, 256x256 PNGs with transparency, and the seamless floor, wall and ceiling
  textures as their own PNGs.
- 8 to 316 triangles a piece, one 1024x1024 texture atlas, shading baked into the vertex colours,
  real-world scale.

### Godot 4.3 to 4.7

An addon with a scene per piece: box colliders on every part (a doorway's leave the way through
clear), an AnimationPlayer where it moves, one lit material so your flashlight falls on it, and
a gallery. Tested on Godot 4.3 and 4.7, on Forward+, Mobile and Compatibility.

```gdscript
# door: the piece in your level
door.get_node("AnimationPlayer").play("open")
```

For the full PS1 look, [PSX Look](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look) turns the kit (and your
level) over in one line: wobbling vertices, warping textures, 240p, dither and 15-bit colour. Its
[free sampler](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look-free) has the vertex snap and affine textures.

For the sound of it, [PSX Horror SFX](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-horror-sfx) has footsteps
on concrete, tile and metal, creaking doors, creatures, stingers and the typewriter save,
PS1-style ([30 free](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-horror-sfx-free)).
